# ODX-FI Motion Principles

Only physical objects possess inertia.

Information is instantaneous.

Hardware has mass.

Motion communicates material.

The ODX-FI behaves like an instrument, not an interface.

---

# Mechanical Motion

The trainer is watching a real instrument rearrange itself for a maintenance task.

Every moving part has a mechanical purpose.

If a component cannot justify its motion as a physical mechanism, it should not move.

---

# Information

Information appears immediately.

Information never waits for animation.

Animation must never delay information.

---

# Hardware

Hardware moves because it occupies space.

Movement reveals function.

The trainer should understand what a component is simply by watching it move.

---

# Motion Rules

The CRT is heavy.

The Service Drawer is spring-loaded.

Buttons retract.

Service controls replace them.

Droidball rolls.

LEDs bloom.

Nothing else moves without purpose.

---

# Physical Consequences

Hardware does not transition.

Hardware reconfigures.

Service Mode does not replace the interface.

It reveals the diagnostic hardware that was already part of the instrument.

The trainer is not changing screens.

The trainer is changing the physical configuration of the ODX-FI. an interface.
