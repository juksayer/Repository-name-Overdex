# Overdex Dictionary
### Architectural Vocabulary Snapshot
**November 2026**

This document records the current meaning of significant Overdex architectural terms. It is a **snapshot** reflecting the working codebase and runtime architecture.

Where this document conflicts with the Constitution or Terms of Ownership, those governance documents prevail. Historical terminology in source code or legacy documentation remains historical evidence.

---

## Article

An **Article** is a subject in Reality about which Overdex acquires measurements, evidence, knowledge, and understanding.

An Article originates in Reality and does not enter Overdex custody directly. Overdex maintains records concerning an Article, but those records are not the Article itself.

```text
Article (in Reality) ≠ Overdex's record concerning the Article
```

**Ownership:** Reality.

---

## Phenomenon

A **Phenomenon** is an occurrence in Reality (e.g. HP bar change, fast move animation, audio cry) that becomes accessible to Overdex through incoming signals.

**Ownership:** Reality.

---

## Crop

A **Crop** is a bounded region of incoming visual signal from which Overdex takes measurements.

The Crop forms the sensory boundary between Reality and Overdex. A Crop does not determine canonical meaning or assign institutional confidence; its measurements enter custody regardless of their immediate interpretation.

```text
Reality ──► Crop ──► Measurement
```

**Ownership:** Sensory boundary.

---

## Measurement

A **Measurement** is an Overdex-produced raw record of something encountered through its sensory boundary.

Measurements are preserved before interpretation. They include recognizer outputs, candidate values, similarity scores, signal properties, timestamps, Crop identity, and provenance.

```text
Measurement ≠ Interpretation
Measurement ≠ Confidence
```

**Ownership:** Overdex.

---

## Testimony (`TestimonyRecord` / `TestimonyPayload`)

**Testimony** is an immutable, typed signal generated by a Witness upon evaluating a Crop or audio signal.

Testimony carries structured payload evidence (e.g., `CropCaptured`, `ActivePokemonHpWitnessed`, `FastMoveUseWitnessed`), wall and monotonic timestamps, observer identity, and content-addressed SHA-256 artifact digests.

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.battle.custody.TestimonyRecord`

---

## Custody (`TestimonyCustody`)

**Custody** is the immutable event storage engine and source availability ledger.

Custody receives testimony and source-availability records without dropping high-rate bursts. It enforces artifact preservation and digest verification before testimony can be published to the Timeline.

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.battle.custody.TestimonyCustody`

---

## Reality Article (`RealityArticle`)

A **Reality Article** is an immutable, timestamped record envelope representing an atomic unit of observed or inferred reality.

Reality Articles wrap testimony records, source availability events, or inference conclusions for preservation in the Reality Timeline.

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.battle.reality.RealityArticle`

---

## Reality Timeline (`RealityTimeline`)

The **Reality Timeline** is the canonical, permanent chronological custodian of what Overdex encounters and infers.

Measurements enter the Timeline **before interpretation**. Later derived conclusions enter as subsequent Articles.

```text
Crop ──► Testimony ──► TestimonyCustody ──► RealityArticle ──► RealityTimeline ──► Inference ──► Timeline
```

Timeline never silently overwrites an earlier record with a later understanding. Contradiction is history and is preserved as history.

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.battle.reality.RealityTimeline`

---

## Evidence

**Evidence** is preserved information used to support, oppose, or inform an interpretation. Evidence describes the role information plays in reasoning, rather than a separate record type.

---

## Interpretation & Inference

An **Interpretation** (or derived **Inference**) is an account of what available evidence means (e.g. `FastMoveUseInference`, `FastMoveEnergyInference`).

Interpretation occurs only after source measurements have entered custody. An interpretation never replaces its supporting measurements.

---

## Confidence

**Confidence** describes how strongly available evidence supports an interpretation.

Confidence belongs to reasoning about evidence—it is not a property of an Article, Phenomenon, or Crop.

```text
Recognizer Score = process output
Confidence       = strength of evidentiary support for an interpretation
```

---

## Reference Knowledge vs. Dynamic Knowledge

* **Reference Knowledge:** Knowledge available independently of the current encounter (e.g., Pokémon species, move sets, type matchups, battle mechanics).
* **Dynamic Knowledge:** Knowledge developed or updated through encounters, measurements, and accumulated history.

---

## Droidball Session (`DroidballSession`)

A **Droidball Session** is the lifecycle scope surrounding zero or more battle attempts. It manages foreground screen projection, overlay presentation, and service state.

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.battle.observation.DroidballSession`

---

## Match (`Match`)

A **Match** is the canonical record of an actual live or recorded Pokémon GO battle. It connects custody testimony to the `RealityTimeline`.

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.battle.observation.Match`

---

## Specimen (`OwnedPokemon`)

A **Specimen** is an individual Pokémon instance owned by the trainer, possessing specific CP, moves, size label (XXS..XXL), shadow/purified/shiny flags, and team assignments.

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.model.OwnedPokemon`

---

## Registration Draft (`RegistrationDraft`)

A **Registration Draft** is a persistent state accumulator that collects OCR field readings, crop evidence, and user confirmations during Pokémon registration scans.

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.data.registration.RegistrationDraft`

---

## Match Archive (`MatchArchive`)

A **Match Archive** is a portable, zipped `.odxmatch` package containing a manifest JSON, reality articles, custody records, and content-addressed PNG/WAV artifacts.

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.battle.archive.MatchArchive`

---

## Match Replay Model (`MatchReplayModel`)

The **Match Replay Model** is a deterministic playback reconstruction engine that parses archived `.odxmatch` packages to render timeline excerpts, energy tracks (`ReplayEnergyTrack`), and team resource states (`ReplayTeamResources`).

**Primary Implementation:** `com.example.overdex.battle.replay.MatchReplayModel`

---

## Presentation & Droidball Overlay (`DroidballOverlayPresentation`)

**Presentation** communicates Overdex's current understanding to the trainer via visual, audio, or haptic interfaces without altering the institutional record.

`DroidballOverlayPresentation` acts as the presentation custodian, displaying real-time battle HUD overlays (HP bars, energy, active combatants).

---

## Intent

**Intent** is the behavioral meaning assigned to user input, independent of the physical input mechanism (touchscreen, D-Pad, terminal keyboard).

```text
Physical Input ──► Intent ──► Action
```

---

# Retired & Superseded Vocabulary

## Observation / Observer
* **Status:** Retired / Superseded.
* **Reason:** "Observation" became overloaded across runtime, OCR, UI, overlays, and architectural layers. Sensory responsibilities are now owned precisely by **Crop**, **Witness**, and **TestimonyRecord**.

## Reporter / Registrar / Publisher
* **Status:** Superseded by Timeline-First Custody.
* **Reason:** Timeline-first preservation (`TestimonyCustody` $\rightarrow$ `RealityTimeline`) eliminates the need for separate gatekeeper components to approve records before they enter permanent history.

# Current Epistemic Flow Summary

```text
Reality
  │
  ├── Article (Subject)
  └── Phenomenon (Event in Reality)
        │
────────┴────────── Sensory Boundary
        │
      Crop
        │
   Measurement / TestimonyRecord
        │
  TestimonyCustody (SHA-256 Artifact Verification)
        │
  RealityArticle ──► RealityTimeline
        │
    Reasoning / Inference
        │
  Interpretation + Confidence
        │
  Presentation (Droidball Overlay HUD)
        │
     Trainer
```
