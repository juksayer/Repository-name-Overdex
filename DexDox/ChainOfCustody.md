## THE ARTICLE'S CHAIN OF CUSTODY

##Article I — Epistemic Foundation & Chain of Custody

The integrity of Overdex depends upon preserving the chain of custody of every Article.

An Article is the constitutional record of a single Event.

Every Event gives rise to exactly one Article.

The Article is the only constitutional record permitted to traverse departmental boundaries.

Departments contribute to an Article.

Departments do not own an Article.

The Article accumulates constitutional history through the chain of custody established by each participating office.

Constitutional Journey
Event

Reality produces Events.

When an Event occurs, an Article is created.

The Article receives:

Article Identifier
Event Timestamp
Event Classification
Chain of Custody

The Event itself remains immutable.

Witness

The Witness receives the active Article.

A Witness provides testimony regarding a single phenomenon.

The Witness may attach:

Testimony
Evidence references
Observation timestamp
Witness identity

The Witness shall never attach:

interpretation
confidence
conclusions
recommendations
canonical meaning

A Witness does not reason.

A Witness does not assign meaning.

Reporter

The Reporter receives the active Article.

The Reporter gathers available testimony.

The Reporter may attach:

collected testimony
witness relationships
testimony organization
reporter custody record

The Reporter shall never:

interpret testimony
resolve conflicts
determine truth
assign confidence

The Reporter prepares the Article.

Nothing more.

Interpreter

The Interpreter receives the prepared Article.

The Interpreter is the first constitutional office permitted to reason.

The Interpreter may attach:

canonical meaning
confidence
derived knowledge
conflicts
uncertainty
interpretations

Reasoning begins here.

Reasoning shall remain distinguishable from evidence.

Publisher

The Publisher receives the interpreted Article.

Publication declares that the Article is complete enough to become part of the permanent institutional record.

The Publisher may attach:

publication timestamp
publication metadata
publication record

The Publisher commits the Article to the Timeline.

Catalog (Timeline)

The Timeline preserves published Articles.

The Timeline owns:

preservation
chronology
retrieval

The Timeline shall never alter a published Article.

Consumers retrieve Articles by checking them out from the Timeline.

Presentation

Presentation consumes Articles retrieved from the Timeline.

Presentation communicates Articles through the current medium.

Presentation may produce:

visual output
audio output
haptic output
future presentation media

Presentation shall never modify an Article.

Presentation shall never reinterpret an Article.

Presentation faithfully communicates the published institutional record.

Constitutional Principle

Every office contributes exactly one constitutional responsibility to an Article.

Every contribution shall become part of the Article's chain of custody.

No office may perform the constitutional responsibility of another.

The trustworthiness of Overdex depends upon preserving this chain from the moment an Event occurs until the Article is presented to the trainer.

All constitutional contributions attached to an Article shall concern that Article's phenomenon
