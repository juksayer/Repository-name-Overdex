THE ARTICLE'S CHAIN OF CUSTODY
Article I — Epistemic Foundation & Chain of Custody
The integrity of Overdex depends upon preserving the chain of custody of every measurement and every contribution derived from it.
Reality remains Reality's. Overdex does not take custody of an Article. Overdex takes custody of the measurements it makes concerning that Article.
An Article is the stable subject to which measurements are attributed and evidence accumulates over time.
The Article itself never enters Overdex custody.
Overdex owns the measurements, records, reasoning, confidence, and derived knowledge it produces concerning an Article.
When Overdex documentation refers to "the Article" as an internal record, it is understood to mean Overdex's accumulated records concerning that Article, not the Article itself.
The Epistemic Boundary
An Article originates outside Overdex.
Nothing crosses the boundary between Reality and Overdex without representation or measurement.
A Crop is a bounded region of incoming signal from which Overdex takes measurements.
The Crop is Overdex's sensory boundary.
No interpretation, confidence, conclusion, recommendation, or canonical meaning shall be introduced before the original measurement is preserved.
Measurement
Every measurement shall concern an identifiable phenomenon.
Every measurement shall preserve sufficient provenance to establish where, when, and how it entered Overdex custody.
A measurement may include:
- measurement identity
- phenomenon identity
- Article attribution, when known
- Crop identity
- timestamp
- raw measured value or signal
- measurement metadata
- provenance
- custody information
A measurement need not be understood before it is preserved.
A measurement need not agree with existing knowledge before it is preserved.
A measurement shall not be discarded merely because its significance is presently unknown.
The future significance of a measurement cannot be known at the time it is made.
Timeline
Every measurement shall enter the Timeline before interpretation.
The Timeline is the first institutional custodian of information produced by Overdex.
The Timeline preserves:
- measurements
- provenance
- chronology
- reasoning
- confidence changes
- derived knowledge
- operational state changes
- relationships between original and derived records
The Timeline shall preserve information in the form in which it was received.
The Timeline shall never alter or replace a preserved record.
Later conclusions shall not overwrite earlier measurements.
Later measurements shall not erase earlier measurements.
Contradiction is history and shall itself be preserved.
If it exists, Overdex records it.
Reasoning
Reasoning begins only after the measurements upon which it operates have been preserved.
Reasoning may produce:
- interpretations
- confidence
- uncertainty
- conflicts
- derived knowledge
- relationships
- conclusions
- predictions
- operational state changes
Reasoning shall remain distinguishable from evidence.
Every derived contribution shall remain traceable to the measurements and phenomena from which it arose.
A conclusion shall never replace its supporting measurements.
When reasoning produces new information, that information itself becomes part of the Timeline.
Thus:
measurement
    ↓
Timeline
    ↓
reasoning
    ↓
Timeline
    ↓
further reasoning
    ↓
Timeline
The historical record therefore preserves not only what Overdex concluded, but what Overdex possessed before reaching that conclusion.
Posterity
Overdex preserves measurements not only for their present purpose, but because future purposes cannot be known.
Information that appears irrelevant today may become meaningful when:
- Reference Knowledge changes
- new recognizers are developed
- new relationships are discovered
- additional Matches provide corroborating evidence
- an opponent's Match History accumulates
- previously unknown patterns become detectable
- future reasoning asks questions that present Overdex does not know to ask
Present interpretation shall therefore never limit the future meaning of preserved evidence.
Overdex preserves what it measures so that future Overdex can understand what present Overdex cannot.
Presentation
Presentation consumes information derived from the institutional record.
Presentation communicates that information through the current medium.
Presentation may produce:
- visual output
- audio output
- haptic output
- future presentation media
Presentation shall never modify the institutional record.
Presentation shall never cause a presented interpretation to replace the measurements from which it was derived.
Presentation communicates what Overdex presently understands while the Timeline preserves what Overdex actually encountered.
Constitutional Principles
Reality remains Reality's.
Overdex does not own or take custody of an Article.
Overdex takes custody of its own measurements concerning an Article.
Every measurement enters the Timeline before interpretation.
Every contribution preserves its provenance and chain of custody.
Reasoning remains distinguishable from evidence.
Derived information remains traceable to the measurements from which it arose.
No architectural office may silently perform the constitutional responsibility of another.
Nothing preserved by the Timeline may be overwritten by later understanding.
Information shall not be discarded merely because its present significance is unknown.
Preserve first. Interpret second. Never allow interpretation to overwrite what was preserved.
The trustworthiness of Overdex depends not merely upon preserving what it knows, but upon preserving enough of what it encountered that its knowledge may later be questioned, reconstructed, extended, or replaced.
