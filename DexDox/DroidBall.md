DroidBall Principle

DroidBall never demands attention.

It only acknowledges interaction.

Its personality comes from subtle mechanical behavior rather than speech or exaggerated animation.

## DroidBall Communication Principle

DroidBall never speaks.

It communicates through motion, light, timing, and physical behavior.

The user should understand its intent without requiring spoken language.

## DroidBall Interaction Principle

DroidBall acknowledges attention.

It never competes for attention.

Repeated interactions should gracefully diminish until no response is given.

The purpose of the DroidBall is to support the trainer's work, not distract from it.

Droidball Interaction Principles
Droidball is not a mascot.

Droidball is a field instrument.

His purpose is to:

Observe
Prepare
Collect
Indicate confidence

He never speaks.

He never explains.

He never celebrates.

He simply performs his work.

Droidball only appears when observation is occurring.

Examples:

✅ Calibration

✅ Live Capture

✅ Registration

✅ Analysis

Not:

❌ Browsing the Pokédex

❌ Reading documentation

❌ Team Builder

❌ Settings

Droidball communicates entirely through behavior.

He never displays dialog.

Instead, he communicates through:

movement
timing
LED color
positioning
confidence
Calibration Sequence

User enters Calibration.

...

Half second pause.

...

Droidball rolls into frame.

Overshoots his destination by approximately 3%.

Settles back.

Brief eye contact.

Amber LED.

Calibration begins.

Observation Sequence

User selects:

Combat Power

Droidball rolls toward the top of the CRT.

The observation frame appears.

Purple.

User drags the screenshot underneath the frame.

Not the frame.

The screenshot.

As alignment improves:

Purple

↓

Purple/Yellow

↓

Yellow

↓

Yellow/Green

↓

Green

When confidence becomes high enough:

Tiny green LED flash.

No sound.

No message.

Frame gently fades.

Droidball moves to the next observation.

Live Capture

User taps Droidball.

He rolls into position.

Amber LED.

Screenshot captured.

Observation occurs.

Green LED.

Registration complete.

Droidball rolls away.

Failure

If confidence cannot be established:

LED remains amber.

No error dialog.

No red X.

The observation simply remains incomplete.

Physical Rules

Movement is heavy.

Purposeful.

He always appears to have momentum.

Stopping is never mathematically perfect.

He overshoots slightly.

Corrects.

Stops.

That tiny imperfection makes him feel like a real machine with mass instead of a UI animation.
