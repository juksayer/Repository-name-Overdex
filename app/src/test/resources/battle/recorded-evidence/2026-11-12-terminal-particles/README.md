# Terminal particle measurement fixtures

These are crops of actual video frames, not original live Overdex observations.
`provenance.json` identifies each original MP4, SHA-256, frame PTS/timebase,
native dimensions, optional full-frame bilinear resize, crop rectangle, output
hash and independent OpenCV reference points. Each sample includes its actual
preceding decoded frame. No frame or event was added to a match archive.

Both the native 816×1808 layout and a capture-like 672×1493 resize are represented.
The resized images are laboratory transformations, not a claim that Android's
capture scaler produces byte-identical pixels.

Cramorant and Leavanny are reviewed final charged KOs. Carbink's living recall,
Roselia's reported concession, and Mismagius's living charged sequence are
counterexamples. Shield, QTE and attacking Pelipper images intentionally retain
positive particle signals: a group of points is **not** a faint, side assignment,
disappearance or HP measurement. Future sequence inference must reject these
competing contexts independently. Do not change the counterexample expectations
to make an unconditional particle-to-faint rule pass.

The source-independent Kotlin measurement is compared with the original
OpenCV connected-component/halo calculation. Both restrict the complete sampled
halo to the supplied crop. Pixels outside that crop are unavailable, rather
than black, background, or an assumed continuation of the glow.
