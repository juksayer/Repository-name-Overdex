## Demonstrated failures

All times below are seconds from the beginning of the 17:10:22 record, whose
monotonic origin is `18002548360611`, not from GO.

1. **Distinct losses merged.** Nine persistent losses on the player's bar at
   36.899, 37.816, 38.265, 38.751, 39.489, 39.909, 40.399, 40.936, and 41.381
   seconds became only seven canonical opponent uses. The 38.265 loss was
   merged with 37.816 (449.6 ms apart); 39.909 was merged with 39.489
   (419.9 ms apart). These separate settled HP levels are not two witnesses of
   one loss. Their exact article IDs, times, and fractions are retained in
   `app/src/test/resources/battle/oct12/sealeo-damage-steps.tsv`.
2. **Recheck mistaken for replacement.** A delayed, unsided announcement entry
   request reopened both species checks. The opponent check at 23.217 seconds
   referred to a 20.783-second cue. It erased the known Sneasel identity and
   flushed a 22.914-second damage tick before its 23.345-second border pulse.
   The two witnesses became separate uses even within the merge window.
3. **Support expired impact too early.** A charge-fill or motion observation
   could anchor a pending cluster before the actual impact. Unrelated newer
   Articles could then flush it before a corroborating flash arrived. Some
   known Incinerate flashes and HP losses were also over 450 ms apart.
4. **One side could lose the shared scan.** Two independent live frame
   collectors shared a tracker retaining only its newest frame. If one advanced
   it, the other could receive null for the preceding frame. Archived samples
   show many one-sided deliveries, although occlusion can also cause these;
   the records cannot establish how many were specifically caused by the race.
5. **Replay repeated the merging error.** Its own 450 ms coalescing could hide
   two canonical uses. It also merged by time without excluding the exact raw
   predecessors already cited by a canonical use.

Sealeo was initially hypothesized as Powder Snow in the second record, then
corrected to Water Gun after the side-located super-effective evidence. The
incorrectly combined loss intervals are one concrete contributor to poor
cadence evidence; they are not proof that all classification failures had the
same cause.

## Changes

- One live source-frame subscriber now measures both bars together and emits
  separate player/opponent testimony and bitmap-free detector samples with the
  same original capture time. Capture does not write additional PNGs, change
  calibration, or enqueue a backlog of Bitmaps.
- A new HP loss or a new pulse onset is a separate hit even if frame jitter
  places it under 450 ms from the preceding hit of the same kind.
- Cross-witness corroboration uses the impact timestamp. Only a trusted move
  identity can widen the phase-offset window for a long move, up to one second;
  an unconfirmed cadence hypothesis cannot widen it. A known duration alone
  never creates uses.
- A bounded 1.2-second delivery grace allows late raw witnesses to join a pending
  use before it is finalized. There are at most 24 pending clusters. This adds
  live counting latency (normally about 1.65 s, up to 2.2 s for the widened
  window, plus delivery/scheduling), while preserving original impact times in
  the Timeline/replay. This is a reliability tradeoff to measure in the next
  live run, not an instantaneous-count guarantee.
- Audio capture is requested on the first raw impact, before that wait, and
  retains the attacker side opposite the damaged HP bar. Later corroboration
  does not request another clip for the same cluster.
- Species scan requests retain accepted identity/cadence/energy context.
  Confirmed faints and actual species changes close an appearance. Unidentified
  context still respects entry boundaries; known player moves do not transfer
  to a mirror opponent.
- Replay keeps distinct canonical uses, excludes their cited raw evidence and
  energy derivations from recounting, and retains uncovered independent hits.
  Old archives are not rewritten or assigned fabricated missing uses.
- A derived MatchEnded now reaches the use inference and flushes its final
  pending hits.

## Remaining acceptance boundaries

A fresh controlled battle must still establish attack count, correct idle
periods, fast-move identity timing, and energy generation. The nine-step
regression proves those preserved losses survive inference; it does not prove
that capture observed every attack in the match.

The secure HUD still creates a black region in entire-screen capture. The
retained 17:10 example has the bars below that region, so the mask cannot explain
all misses. A bar crossing the mask remains genuinely unavailable.

None of these three records contains MatchEnded, despite later source images
showing YOU WIN. The third result watcher was operating around 55.977–88.824 s.
Compact archives can omit uncited outcome crops; absence of those crop Articles
is not proof that the witness was stopped. This result-recognition issue remains
open and was not declared fixed by the Fast Move work.

For the next Team Leader run, include a clearly remembered five-second idle
stretch followed by attacking, preferably with a short screen recording. That
checks both false positives during inactivity and missed/duplicated hits during
combat. Existing archive canonical counts should not be treated as ground truth.
