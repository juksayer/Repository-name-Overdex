## Confirmed accounting defects

### One Dragon Pulse produced two energy-spend Articles

At 154.943 and 155.439 s, OCR reads the same ongoing announcement as:

```text
Turtonator used Dragon Pulse
Turtonator used Dragon Pulse!
```

Both produce `ChargedMoveEnergySpent(PLAYER, Turtonator, Dragon Pulse, 55)`.
That is two 55-energy spend Articles 0.497 s apart for one announcement.
`ChargedMoveAnnouncementInference` resolves each raw Article independently and
has no encounter/announcement deduplication. Raw OCR variants should remain
preserved, while one charged action should have only one derived energy spend.

### Opponent charges generated false player QTE inferences

QTE patterns at 73.968 and 115.378 s are marked `PLAYER`. Their predecessor chains
end in generic charge announcements whose cited raw text is **Sealeo used Body
Slam!** at 76.856 and 116.738 s respectively. Both patterns lack a Get Ready cue.
The [source image near the second pattern](evidence/2026-10-12-1546/frame-116.png)
shows the incoming-attack sequence and “No Protect Shields remaining!”

`ChargeMoveQteVibrationInference` allows a generic charge announcement plus three
short device-motion pulses to infer player milestones without checking the
performer's side. These are accelerometer measurements, not confirmed Android
vibration callbacks. Preserve them as raw motion evidence; do not present these
two groups as the player's Nice/Great/Excellent progression.

### Hidden shields were interpreted as spent shields

- Opponent count: 2 → 0 at 75.252 s → 2 at 79.630 s.

The cited [player zero-count crop](evidence/2026-10-12-1546/player-shields-74.png)

## Remaining capture limitation and live acceptance

The user confirmed the Moondrop sharing dialog offers **Entire screen only**.
The Droidball overlay uses `FLAG_SECURE`; the resulting black rectangle in the
captured image removes battlefield pixels beneath the HUD. Expanding or moving
the HP search cannot recover those pixels. Removing that flag would expose the
HUD to recognition instead. This follow-up did not change that capture behavior.
Android documents the blank-screenshot behavior of
[`FLAG_SECURE`](https://developer.android.com/security/fraud-prevention/activities)
and the separate
[app-screen-sharing option](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/14/features/app-screen-sharing).

API 34's
[`takeScreenshotOfWindow`](https://developer.android.com/reference/android/accessibilityservice/AccessibilityService#takeScreenshotOfWindow(int,java.util.concurrent.Executor,android.accessibilityservice.AccessibilityService.TakeScreenshotCallback))
can capture a window without accessibility-overlay interference, but the
[Android 14 implementation](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/base/+/android-14.0.0_r1/services/accessibility/java/com/android/server/accessibility/AbstractAccessibilityServiceConnection.java)
enforces a 333 ms minimum interval per window. That is unsuitable as the primary
lane for brief HP-border pulses. It was not implemented in this follow-up.

A new live match is still required to assess HP continuity, Fast Move recall,
QTE attribution, shields, and result capture together. HUD occlusion remains an
explicit source of missing observations until capture can exclude the overlay
or the HUD can stay outside the relevant battlefield area. The existing archive
was retained unchanged; these fixes do not repair its missing observations.
