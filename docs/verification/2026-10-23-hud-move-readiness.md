# HUD move readiness and confirmations

Charged move labels become amber when the HUD's estimated energy plus one assumed Fast Move's energy gain meets their cost, and bright red when estimated energy already meets the cost. Missing costs or unknown energy leave normal styling. Missing Fast Move gain cannot imply one move away. The existing approximation label remains; readiness is presentation, not observed energy testimony.

Move confirmation remains independent of readiness. Evidence-confirmed Fast Moves survive provisional candidate pruning and switches until a new resolved identity replaces them or the match resets. Punctuation-normalized names match reference entries. Tie-break assumptions never earn checkmarks. The live published evidence collector also forwards FastMoveIdentified and opponent ChargedMoveEnergySpent to HUD confirmation, covering published derivations beyond the direct announcement path.

Regression checks cover readiness boundaries and missing inputs, punctuation matching, energy propagation, switch persistence, candidate pruning, and explicit identity replacement. Physical live-battle color/checkmark acceptance remains to be exercised on the phones.
