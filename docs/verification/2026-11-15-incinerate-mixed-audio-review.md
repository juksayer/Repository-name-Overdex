## Competing sounds and living-switch controls

The same experimental two-fragment probe found no Incinerate in isolated Water Gun, Powder Snow or silence. It detected both Incinerate and Water Gun in the same Sealeo and Vaporeon clips. In a different, previously reviewed Moondrop recording (`Moondrop-2026-11-12_16-05-21-wzGrCr.mp4`, SHA-256 `3be83deb91b54b078f7047e7e3e66618dff47ca2a804d5c6a7d2bd7c7afdaf45`), 22 overlapping windows covered a nonlethal charged hit, nonlethal shield, and both player and opponent living-switch cases. Four of the five windows around the living opponent switch matched Incinerate: a move sound is not faint evidence, and the windows are overlapping, not four independent uses. None of the other reviewed control windows matched Incinerate under this probe.

An additional adversarial pure-reference sweep found one false Incinerate match in Hidden Power at .1402/.144. Of 78 non-Incinerate reference entries, 25 were too short for the 1.2-second query and were skipped. The remaining references and these two sound tracks are not a representative full deployment corpus. The observed false match and skipped samples mean this threshold is **not suitable for production** and the mixed-audio experiment has not passed a broad false-positive test. Do not silently tune a threshold to these two KOs or use the probe to publish moves or KO credit.

The existing capture-time, mirror, competing-move, living-switch, shield and replay safeguards were rerun: 55 focused tests passed, none skipped. These are tests of existing inference guards, not proof that the experimental probe is implemented or that Replay now associates these KOs. The 1 TB review folder contains `audio_clock_compare.py`, `video_audio_compare.py`, `fragment_coherence.py`, JSON results and `fragment-coherence.log`; the scripts use NumPy and operate on derived audio copies. The raw WAV clips remain inside the unchanged source archive. No APK was changed or installed in this investigation.

## Implementation gap

Completed Replay's existing retained-audio selection emphasizes the final 40 seconds and earlier entry windows. It can miss Sealeo's mid-match finishing audio; its stricter single-fragment waveform comparison did not recover these mixed Incinerates. The live spectral matcher ranked Water Gun ahead of Incinerate in several Sealeo windows, and the Vaporeon window had incomplete opponent search scope, so neither became an accepted player Fast Move. Replay currently has automatic finishing-charge association but no equivalent automatic Fast Move KO association. A safe implementation needs a calibrated mixed-sound detector with diverse short and mixed competing sounds, source-time identity and switch guards, distinct-occurrence deduplication, a separately supported faint, and a link to the matching appearance. Its output must remain revisable and retain the source clips and evidence; the known Hidden Power false positive is a required regression.
