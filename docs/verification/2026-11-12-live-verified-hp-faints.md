# Live verified-HP faint integration

The strict normal-field RED → EMPTY outline rule now feeds the live faint
publisher as well as completed Replay. It uses `VerifiedHpFaintResolver`; no
expected damage, missing pixels, result screen, or cry is substituted for an
empty outline. Centered charged-impact bars remain ineligible for this path.

`LiveVerifiedHpFaintHistory` retains original source times, dimensions, geometry,
confidence and identity provenance. Delayed identities and HP measurements are
reconciled in capture order. Conflicting custody copies are unknown barriers.
Match bounds and manual restarts exclude ineligible attempts/frames. Ordinary
positive HP frames after a settled conclusion do not repeatedly sort and replay
the entire HP history. Tests check work counts, not a claimed phone speedup.

The faint publisher cites identity, red and empty articles and uses the empty
frame's capture clock even when recognition completes later. Current source
history combines independent count and HP conclusions. Count measurements stay
unchanged. A later count can publish independent corroboration; presentation and
metrics reconcile actors rather than tallying raw faint articles.

A fresh independent badge naming the same actor after the empty frame, followed
by positive HP, retracts the current HP inference. The live opponent badge,
Fast Move use ownership, cadence identity, energy timing and opponent energy
forecast can recover. The old inference and original measurements stay in the
ledger. Replay, match summaries and performance metrics recompute the current
conclusion and do not retain that obsolete faint. A new name alone, or HP with
only the old name, cannot restore the actor.

Consumers with permanent state do not promote HP inference into independent
proof: it cannot retire a player roster slot, assign a trainer side to an unsided
entry, or stop independent audio by contributing to the five-faint cutoff.
Charge-button/announcement matching also checks whether an HP faint still has
support. A later count corroborating an already recorded HP KO cannot pause the
replacement's energy forecast a second time.

## Evidence and acceptance boundary

The integration tests remeasure the retained Tinkaton red and empty video crops
using the real pixel reader, then vary delivery order and identity timing.
These are the same documented scaled 672×1493 fixtures used by the earlier
Replay tests, not new measurements recovered from the original live archive.
Assigning those pixels to the player side verifies plumbing symmetry, not a
second recorded player KO.

Tests also exercise corrected HUD state, archived-article immutability, summary
and performance correction, delayed pre-KO hits, resumed use/cadence/energy,
duplicate counts, independent audio, wrong-camera/unknown-identity negatives,
conflicting copies, restarts, and avoiding repeated HP/forecast rebuilds.

The final Cramorant/Rock Tomb sequence still has no recorded EMPTY observation.
Its disappearing bar and shrinking sprite require separate terminal-visual
evidence. This integration does not invent that faint or set its HP to zero.
