# Mixed Fast Move audio in completed Replay

Replay can retain more than one possible Fast Move sound in the same captured clip. Each mixed candidate contains the source audio article ID and hash, two nonoverlapping reference fragments, their offsets and scores, and an estimated onset. Repeated overlapping captures with the same move and onset are grouped without deleting their fragments. The onset can precede the captured clip when the reference fits a later fragment; it is an estimate, not an observed initial sample. Capture timestamps and archives remain unchanged.

The bounded completed-archive selector now also looks near an earlier repeat-cry departure candidate, making the archived Sealeo clip eligible alongside the end-of-match Vaporeon clip. The matcher examines the move names preserved in the clip's spectral candidate list. It compares independent 400 ms reference segments at one onset, retaining simultaneous candidates. Its thresholds are provisional and the resulting evidence is a **candidate**, not a confirmed move, side, impact, faint or HP value. It does not replace live inference or the existing stricter audio recognizer.

Replay's finishing-Fast-Move association requires an independently supported and named Fast Move cycle, a separate resolved faint, a compatible source-timed mixed-audio candidate, and compatible attacker and recipient appearances. It rejects nearby independent uses that could claim the same sound, intervening player attacks, known positive surviving HP, absent ownership, and same-species mirror ambiguity. A delayed count may use a matching cry for an earlier association anchor, but the cry cannot establish the faint. A supported link is attached to the Replay action, combatant and Fast Move marker with audio, move and faint evidence IDs. It never creates an attack or faint from audio alone.
