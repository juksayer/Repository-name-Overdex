   Existing score/margin, completeness, context and side guards remain required.
2. Completed Replay compares captured onset/effect clocks without imposing the
   live publication budget. Existing source alignment, phase, appearance and
   duplicate checks remain. Live queue retention has not changed.
3. A count-resolved loss may use earlier independent HP proof for that same
   appearance's fall. Both evidence chains remain available, one marker is
   rendered, and the resource count keeps its previous presentation time.

The recovered effect precedes the empty HP frame by about 2.08 seconds. This
test establishes a supported attack in the finishing exchange and a supported

the archive. The 500 ms HP shortcut was deliberately not widened: the archive
does not contain every frame-hub input or its eligibility state.

Correction from the trainer, November 13: a final tick/empty outline is not a
universal prerequisite. Some Fast and Charged Move finishes show a change in the
remaining fill's color followed by fading. The next test must cover two source
patterns: (a) visible depletion/empty outline and (b) terminal color transition
plus departure with no empty frame. Absence of a tick is not itself a failed KO
test. HP-11 records the candidate signal and counterexamples.

For pattern (b), review native video frames around the known Cramorant Rock Tomb
finish, a Fast Move finish and the shield-delayed finish. Measure fill colors,

to label examples; do not label them from the same color signal under test.
Record an uncertain time interval if the video cannot expose the exact impact.

without manufacturing an observed zero frame or an exact final damage amount.
