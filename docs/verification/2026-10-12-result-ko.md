## Publication delay and the HUD

The preceding Fast Move change adds a 1.2-second evidence-delivery grace, in
addition to the correlation window: normally about 1.65 seconds and up to 2.2
seconds for the widened window, plus processing/delivery. Canonical attack
counts and energy inherit that delay. Move identification can inherit it when
it depends on counted attacks; direct cadence/effectiveness/audio evidence also
feeds identification. This turn does not change that waiting period.

Accepted species, possible movepools, faint marks, and charged-move confirmations
update DroidballOverlayPresentation directly and do not pass through that Fast
Move wait. Other recognition or delivery delays can still affect their arrival.
The Timeline keeps original impact times for replay.
